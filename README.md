# Humanity-
A social media platform that's not about influencers,  popularity, brands, opinions, or competition. It's for ordinary people who love to share glimpses of their lives, without making it a habit or   another form of stress.

I want to address something. People may ask, "What if someone uses Humanity and never comes back?"

Well, that's the purpose. Humanity is for those who enjoy participating simply for the sake of participating, not for those seeking constant stimulation or dopamine-driven engagement.

The doors are always open. People are free to enter, stay, leave, or return whenever they wish. There is no intention to keep anyone attached and hooked up.
