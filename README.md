# Humanity-
A social media platform that's not about influencers,  popularity, brands, opinions, or competition. It's for ordinary people who love to share glimpses of their lives, without making it a habit or   another form of stress.

# Personal problem: What am I facing?

These are some of the things I have personally noticed social media becoming over time:

* Addictive scrolling
* Personalized recommendation
* Social metrics
* Pressure to maintain an identity
* Inauthenticity
* Attention seeking
* Information overload
* Commercialization
* FOMO
* Loss of Spontaneity
* Permanent digital footprints
* Loss of simplicity
* Need for engagement
